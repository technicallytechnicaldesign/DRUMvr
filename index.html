How to use this first version

Pick the pulse exercise if you are starting from zero. Listen once, then turn off guide drums and play the lit targets. Swing the controller sticks to strike drums. Keyboard and pad buttons also play each drum.

In VR, swing either controller like a drumstick to hit the black drumheads. Either index trigger plays kick. A or X starts/stops practice. B or Y recenters the kit in front of you and resets its angle. Point at the floating LEFT / RIGHT / RESET buttons and press the trigger to turn the kit and score together in 15° steps. Left thumbstick forward/back changes reach; right thumbstick forward/back changes kit height.

Open this HTTPS page in Quest Browser and select Enter VR. The kit aligns around your headset on entry. Use B/Y to recenter if needed. The new rotation controls are ready for a headset check.
